2. MATHEMATICAL CHAIN INSPECTED

(a) Padding: any covering with at most 29 blocks can be enlarged to exactly 29
distinct seven-subsets; there are 1,716 possible blocks. No coverage is lost.

(b) Minimum degrees: the link at any point covers all triples on twelve points.
The published bound C(12,6,3) >= 15 gives degree at least 15. Total degree is
29*7 = 203 = 13*15+8, forcing at least five points of degree 15.

(c) Root normalization: remove one degree-15 root. Its link is a classified
15-block cover and exactly 14 blocks remain outside the root. All 495 choices
of four other points are considered for every one of the 68 root types.

(d) Domain completeness: for another degree-15 point, its entire link is also
classified. Every source center and every full star-preserving bijection is
allowed. Non-root source blocks give precisely ALL outside blocks through
that point. Taking only a convenient subpencil would be invalid; the definition
and both reconstructed implementations use complete pencils.

(e) Cache transport: one actual star isomorphism suffices to transport an
already complete domain. Composition and inverse composition give a bijection
on all source embeddings. Coarse invariant equality alone never suffices.
This justification has been made explicit in the edited manuscript.

(f) Pair consistency: actual pencils agree exactly on blocks through each pair
of selected points. Arc deletion removes a value only if it has no possible
partner in another domain. A genuine compatible assignment survives every round
by induction. Pair and arc stages are necessary-condition filters only.

(g) Last join: the independent six-relation join applies no union-budget pruning.
Every compatible residual tuple has union cardinality 15, exceeding the outside
budget 14. No final covering sufficiency claim is needed: failure of a necessary
condition already gives the contradiction.

(h) The upper construction is previously known and credited. The literal TeX
table was parsed and all 715 quadruples checked independently. Removing each
one of the 30 listed blocks destroys coverage, supplying 30 negative controls.
